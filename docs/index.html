02

Information Collection and Use

Hangman is designed as an offline word puzzle game. We believe great games do not require personal surveillance or invasive data collection.

A. Personal Identifiable Information (PII)

We do NOT collect, store, or share any personal identifiable information, including but not limited to:

  • Your name, physical address, or phone number
  • Email addresses or account credentials (the app requires no registration or login)
  • Precise or coarse GPS location
  • Financial, banking, or payment information
  • Biometric, facial recognition, or health data

B. Local Game Storage (Device-Only)

To provide core game functionality and save your progress between sessions, the application utilizes your device's built-in local key-value storage (via Flutter shared_preferences). This includes:

  • Game Statistics: Number of games played, total wins, losses, current win streak, and best win streak.
  • Audio Preferences: Toggle states for background music and sound effects (muted/unmuted).
  • Category Progress: Unlocked word categories (Animals, Countries, Food, Movies, Sports, Technology).

Important Note: All game data is stored exclusively on your physical device. It is never uploaded to any remote server, cloud storage, or external database by M S ELECTRONICS.

03

Device Permissions & Hardware Access

Hangman is built with a minimal permission footprint to ensure complete security:

  • No Sensitive Permissions: The app does not request access to your Camera, Microphone, Photo Library, Contacts, Calendar, or Location.
  • No Background Services: The application runs only when you actively launch and play it, and does not execute persistent background monitoring services.
  • Audio Playback: Uses the device's standard audio output to play sound effects and optional music. No recording capability is included or requested.

Children’s Privacy (COPPA & Families Compliance)

Protecting the privacy of young children is critically important. Hangman is an educational word puzzle game suitable for all ages and fully complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play’s Families Policy.

06

Data Security & Storage Architecture

Because we do not maintain central web servers or remote databases collecting user records, your exposure to data breaches or online leaks through this application is eliminated.

All local game preference files reside in the sandboxed private application directory provided by the Android operating system. Other applications installed on your device cannot access Hangman’s private local settings.

07

Data Retention & User Control (Deletion)

You have complete autonomy over the data stored on your device:

  • Reset Statistics: You can reset your win/loss statistics and scores directly within the game's settings screen.
  • Clear App Data: You can clear all cached game states at any time by going to your Android device's Settings > Apps > Hangman > Storage > Clear Data.
  • Uninstalling the App: Uninstalling Hangman permanently deletes all locally stored preferences, scores, and records from your device immediately.
